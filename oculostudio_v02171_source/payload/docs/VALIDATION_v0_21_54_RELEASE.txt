Key findings:
- Playback advances immediately through a compact rolling cache; video, 2D overlay, 3D body evidence and gaze ray stay frame-locked.
- The full paused scientific inspector is restored after playback stops.
- Semantic queue reduction can clear raw-surface disagreements only when person, entity and exported coarse body label are unchanged.
- Self occlusion, person/entity conflicts and different coarse body regions remain human-review decisions.
- Adjacent target-stable fixation units can be presented as one review episode without deleting original gaze samples.

Scope and limitations:
- Playback and semantic queue-reduction validations use deterministic synthetic OculoStudio projects so frame movement and safety boundaries are reproducible.
- Core Body/Face review, torso geometry, gaze-sample-clock and result-handoff paths were regression-tested against the current source tree.
- The exact reduction for the user’s real Body AOI artifact cannot be inferred from screenshots; the re-evaluation job reports its actual before/after event and sample counts.
- Existing manual decisions are protected; review-only re-evaluation must not silently discard completed human work.
- Windows batch installation is statically checked here; final Windows execution occurs on the user’s machine.
